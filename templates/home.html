How a rank is built

Medical fit first, chemistry second

01 Hard stop

A gene both profiles list as a confirmed recessive carrier blocks that donor. The conflict stays on the card, so you always see why.

02 Soft weights

ID release, family limit, CMV, and quarantine change the score. Rh is a flag, not a block.

03 Context only

Ancestry overlap is allele-frequency context and does not change the rank. There is no race filter. Hair, eyes, height, and BMI can narrow the deck but never change the rank.

What we do not do

  • Predict or generate a future child's face or appearance. Baby photos show the donor as a child.
  • Rank donors by IQ, personality, attractiveness, or other polygenic traits
  • Claim to diagnose disease or replace clinical genetic counseling
  • Use ancestry or race as a match-quality score

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Whose baby photo is on each card?

The donor's own childhood photo, as sperm banks publish them. It is never a prediction of your child. In this demo every photo is a synthetic stand-in.

Does swiping change the medical rank?

No. Swiping right adds a donor to your shortlist, and swiping left hides the card for now. The rank comes only from carrier status, blood type, and your survey.

Can I still shortlist a blocked donor?

Yes. A carrier conflict stays visible on the card, and you can keep that donor on the shortlist to discuss with a counselor.

What does the paywall unlock?

Intake forms are free. A one-time payment unlocks the donor match deck and explanations for a couple account.

What goes to the AI explanation?

Only the retrieved match fields. Clinical free text stays out of model context, and missing facts are stated as not in the record.
