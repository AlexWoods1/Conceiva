Donor

Your path is the bank's donor record

Conceiva does not have a separate donor login. The sperm bank holds the application, screening, sample analysis, and clearance on your donor record. When that record is cleared, intended parents can see it in Discover.

This indicator is the shape of the record. It is not your personal status.

Application

The bank creates the donor record: code, blood type, Rh, ancestry context, and ID-release policy.

Screening

Carrier results, CMV, the screening panel name, and quarantine live on that same record. A gene you share with an intended parent is a hard stop on their list. It is not hidden.

Samples

The bank can upload a microscopy clip. The existing analysis reports total and progressive motility against WHO lower reference limits. A result below those limits is flagged for lab review. It does not by itself remove the donor.

Status

Cleared, confirmed records appear in intended-parent discovery. Family limit on the record is the cap the bank entered. Individual vials, shipments, and clinic appointments are not stored yet.
